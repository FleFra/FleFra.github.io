About Me

Hi, I’m Fleur — a 22-year-old software development student with a passion for cars, video games, and art. I'm currently a student at ROC van Twente in Enschede. At school we mostly focus on game development in Unity and c#. I also enjoy the creative side of game development; I know my way around Blender and Aseprite. Besides Game development I also have a love for photography and cars. I love going for drives and constantly fixing up the various ailments on my 26 year old Toyota Celica.
